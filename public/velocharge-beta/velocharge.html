Help
How do I start charging?
Tap a station on the map, check availability, and use "Pay with VeloValue" to start a session. You'll need a linked payment method.
What do the colors mean?
Green dots = confirmed operational. Amber dots = status unknown or other. Clustered circles show multiple stations grouped together.
Data is wrong or outdated?
Use "Submit Feedback" below to report inaccuracies. We sync with OpenChargeMap regularly.
Why can't I see my location?
Make sure you've allowed location access in your browser. Check your browser's address bar for a location permission icon.
