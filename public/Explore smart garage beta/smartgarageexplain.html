Smart Garage

Give your car a premium digital home

Upload documents once, get timely reminders, and keep the entire history of your vehicle at your fingertips. Smart Garage combines security, convenience and intelligence — so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time driving.

Why it matters

Never miss renewals or service dates. Keep documents secure and accessible when you need them — for insurance, resale, or maintenance.

How it works

Add your vehicle with the number plate, verify ownership, upload documents once and let Smart Garage handle reminders and organization.

Privacy first

GDPR-aware and encrypted at rest. We store what you choose — and nothing is shared without your consent.

All your car details, one click away

Upload insurance, PUC, service receipts and photos. Find everything in a timeline that makes sense — ready when you need it for claims, resale or maintenance.

Your garage, now digital

Access your vehicle's history anywhere, anytime. Export records when you sell, show proof for warranty, or share service receipts with your mechanic.

Future-ready intelligence

Smart Garage isn't just storage — it learns patterns and surfaces helpful reminders, predicted service needs, and suggestions to protect your car's value.
